# Application Materials & Language Waivers (申请材料与语言豁免)

> Illustrative guide to typical master's application files in France and Europe.
> Requirements vary per program — the program page always wins.

## Standard document set

Almost every European master's application asks for: passport copy, transcripts (all
years, translated), degree/graduation certificates, CV (1–2 pages, Europass accepted but
not required), motivation letter, two recommendation letters, and language test proof.
Selective programs add: GRE/GMAT (mostly business & top engineering), portfolio
(design/architecture), or a research proposal (research master's / PhD).

## Motivation letter (动机信) expectations

One page, program-specific. Structure that works: why this field → what you have done
(projects, internships, research with concrete results) → why THIS program (name courses,
labs, tracks) → career plan after graduation. Cross-discipline applicants must connect
their original major to the target field explicitly — list relevant coursework, MOOCs,
and projects. Generic letters are the most common rejection driver among borderline
candidates.

## Recommendation letters (推荐信)

Two letters, at least one academic. Choose referees who know your work, not just famous
names. Many portals email referees directly — warn them 4–6 weeks before the deadline and
give them your CV + draft SOP. Industry referees are appropriate if you have 2+ years of
work experience or the internship was substantial.

## Language test waivers (语言豁免政策)

Common waiver grounds (varies by school):

- Your ENTIRE prior degree was taught in English — request a "medium of instruction"
  certificate from your university. Widely accepted in the Netherlands, Ireland, and at
  many French grandes écoles; less often at German technical universities.
- You are a citizen of an English-speaking country.
- You completed a substantial exchange (1+ year) in an English-speaking country — accepted
  only by some schools.

Waivers rarely apply to French-taught programs: DELF/DALF/TCF at the stated CEFR level is
almost always mandatory. If your IELTS is 0.5 below the bar, some schools offer
conditional admission with a pre-sessional language course — ask admissions instead of
skipping the application.

## Deadlines and rolling admission

Grandes écoles and Dutch universities often run multiple rounds — earlier rounds have
better scholarship odds. Public French universities via "Mon Master" have one fixed
national window (spring). Rolling-admission programs (common in Ireland) can close early
when full: apply as soon as your file is complete, not at the deadline.

## Common missing-item pitfalls (缺失材料)

Top reasons files are marked incomplete: missing certified translation, referee never
submitted the letter, language report not sent through the official channel (IELTS TRF
verification), missing degree-equivalence note for 3-year bachelors, and scholarship
forms submitted after the (earlier) scholarship deadline.
