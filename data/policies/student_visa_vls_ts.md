# French Long-Stay Student Visa (VLS-TS)

> Illustrative reference document for the demo knowledge base. Verify against France-Visas
> and your local consulate before acting.

## What is the VLS-TS

The VLS-TS ("visa de long séjour valant titre de séjour") is the long-stay student visa that
also acts as a residence permit for stays longer than six months. Once validated after
arrival, it lets you live and study in France for the duration of the visa.

## Core documents typically required

- Valid passport covering the length of stay.
- Campus France / "Études en France" validation (where the procedure applies).
- Proof of enrolment or admission from a recognised institution.
- Proof of sufficient financial resources — commonly around 615 EUR per month for the
  planned stay (amount is indicative and updated periodically).
- Proof of accommodation for at least the first months.
- A completed long-stay visa application form and recent photographs.
- Visa fee payment (student rate).

## Validation after arrival

Within three months of arriving in France you must validate the VLS-TS online through the
official ANEF / immigration portal, pay the residence-permit tax, and provide your French
address. Failure to validate on time can make your stay irregular.

## Renewal

To renew, apply at your local prefecture (or online) before the current permit expires,
usually two to three months ahead. You will typically need proof of academic progress,
continued financial resources, and accommodation.

## Working while studying

A student residence permit generally allows part-time work up to a legal annual limit
(historically 60% of the full-time working year). Earnings should not replace proof of
financial resources at the visa stage.
