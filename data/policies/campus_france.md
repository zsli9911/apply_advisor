# Campus France Procedure (Études en France)

> Illustrative reference document for the demo knowledge base. Always verify against the
> official Campus France website for your country before relying on any detail.

## What is Campus France

Campus France is the official agency that manages applications from international students
to French higher-education institutions. Students from many countries must go through the
"Études en France" (EEF) online platform before they can apply for a student visa.

## Who must use the "Études en France" procedure

Students who are nationals of, or residents in, one of the ~70 countries connected to the
"Études en France" procedure must create an EEF account and submit their application there.
Nationals of the EU/EEA and Switzerland are generally exempt and apply directly to the
institution.

## Steps in the procedure

1. Create an "Études en France" account with your personal and academic information.
2. Build your electronic file: diplomas, transcripts, language certificates, CV, and a
   motivation letter for each program.
3. Select up to a fixed number of programs (typically up to 7) and pay the Campus France
   processing fee.
4. Attend a pedagogical interview at your local Campus France office (in person or online).
5. Receive admission decisions from the institutions through the platform.
6. Confirm your acceptance, then Campus France validates your file for the visa stage.

## Timeline

The main campaign usually opens in the autumn (around October–November) for the following
academic year, with a first-phase deadline in the winter. Plan for the language test and
document translation well in advance, because certified translations can take several weeks.

## Fees

A non-refundable Campus France processing fee applies in most countries. The amount varies
by country; check your local office. This is separate from any application fee charged by the
institution itself and from the later visa fee.
