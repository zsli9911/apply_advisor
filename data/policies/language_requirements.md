# Language Requirements and Certificates

> Illustrative reference document for the demo knowledge base.

## English-taught programs

Programs taught in English usually require proof of English proficiency unless you are a
native speaker or completed a prior degree in English. Commonly accepted tests:

- IELTS Academic — typical master's requirement is an overall band of 6.5, sometimes 7.0 for
  selective programs, with per-section minimums.
- TOEFL iBT — typical requirement between 80 and 100 depending on selectivity.
- Some schools accept Duolingo English Test or Cambridge C1 Advanced.

Check whether a program waives the test for applicants whose previous instruction was in
English; policies differ.

## French-taught programs

Programs taught in French generally require a CEFR level of B2 or higher. Accepted proofs
include:

- DELF B2 / DALF C1 (official diplomas, valid for life).
- TCF (Test de Connaissance du Français) — a score-based test valid for a limited period.

Some grandes écoles require C1 for humanities and social-science tracks.

## Planning your test

Book language tests early: seats fill up, and results can take two to four weeks. Ensure the
test date and validity window still cover your application and visa timeline. For French
diplomas like DELF/DALF, the certificate does not expire, which makes them convenient if you
plan ahead.
