# Scholarships for Studying in France and Europe

> Illustrative reference document for the demo knowledge base.

## Eiffel Excellence Scholarship

Funded by the French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs, the Eiffel programme supports
international students at master's and PhD level in priority fields (science, engineering,
economics/management, law, political science). Applications are submitted by the host
institution, not by the student directly, so you must contact your target school early. The
scholarship provides a monthly allowance and covers certain travel and insurance costs;
tuition is not automatically covered.

## Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM)

Erasmus Mundus funds prestigious joint master's programs delivered by consortia of European
universities, usually with study periods in two or more countries. Selected students receive
a generous scholarship covering tuition, travel, and living costs. Applications go directly to
the individual EMJM program, each with its own deadline (often between December and February).

## French Government Scholarships (BGF)

Bourses du Gouvernement Français are managed through French embassies and Campus France in
each country. Coverage and selection vary widely by country and program.

## Institution-specific scholarships

Many schools offer their own merit or need-based awards — for example excellence grants at
grandes écoles, or reduced income-based tuition at public universities. Check each program's
funding page and apply within the admission cycle, as some awards require a separate form.

## Tips

Start at least 8–12 months before the intake. Most competitive scholarships close months
before the program's own deadline, and many require reference letters and a dedicated
motivation statement.
